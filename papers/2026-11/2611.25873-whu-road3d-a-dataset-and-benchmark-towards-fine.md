# WHU-Road3D: A dataset and benchmark towards fine-grained road surface mapping

> **arXiv**：[2611.25873](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25873) · **主题**：`理论、评测与数据` · **发表**：2026-11-19 · **发表状态**：arXiv 预印本

> 状态：每日精读（智能体）

## 一句话结论
发布约 98km、覆盖中国三区城市道路与高速的 MLS 点云/全景/轨迹数据集，含 15 类路面要素的矢量化和逐点标注；基准远未饱和：0.3m 容差下 MapTR 人行横道轮廓 F1 仅 0.292、路面边界 0.473。

## 为什么值得读（观点）
现有 nuScenes/Argoverse 只有少数类别且以图像为主；这里多了 10 类地面方向符号、虚实线区分和三种人行横道粒度，还同时给矢量化与逐点实例标注，是地图级结构化感知少见的评测床。

## 问题与动机
LoD2 数据在语义、模态、场景复杂度上不足；点级标签难监督矢量化建图；方向符号对分层路网拓扑重要却被忽略；城市遮挡与磨损让点云残缺，跨城市泛化未知。

## 方法与关键洞察
用 MLS 系统同步全景相机采集，按 30m 缓冲与最近轨迹高程裁剪；标注分矢量化线/多边形、逐点语义、逐点实例三层，遮挡数据洞按整体几何连续标注，避免把缺失当负样本；基线投影成三通道 BEV，跑 MapTR-tiny、WHU-Lane 与 6-DoF 标线检测。

## 证据与实验
训练 SH+CM、测试 WH，0.3m 容差：MapTR 实线 F1 0.727、虚线 0.773、路面边界 0.473、人行横道轮廓 0.292；WHU-Lane 实线 0.801、虚线 0.842；斑马线条 0.951、前进箭头 0.931，但停止线 0.688、菱形 mIoU 0.437。

## 可质疑之处
①只给跨区划分（SH+CM→WH），城市、传感器、场景同时改变，无同区随机划分，测到的是泛化而非数据难度；②基线仅 3 个且类别不重叠；③缺标注一致性与几何精度证据；④曲线箭头全库仅 6 个。

## 与我的研究关联
做 3D 分割、点云-图像融合或 HD 地图可当结构化监督实验床；做语音数据集可借鉴“数据洞连续标注+多粒度标注”与跨域划分隐患；只做 TTS/ASR 则优先级最低。

nuScenes、OpenLane-V2、MapTRv2、WHU-Lane；github.com/WHU-USI3DV/WHU-Road3D。

<summary>展开英文摘要</summary>

Accurately extracting and mapping road surface elements is crucial for applications such as high-definition maps, road asset management, and intelligent transportation systems. This study first provides a comprehensive review of the benchmark datasets for extracting and mapping road elements, encompassing overhead-to-perspective data, large-to-local scales, and coarse-to-fine details. Subsequently, we thoroughly review the relevant approaches for extracting road surface elements. However, we argue that existing datasets still require enhancements in semantic richness, data modality diversity, and scenario complexity. To bridge these gaps, this paper presents WHU-Road3D, a large-scale multimodal dataset for fine-grained road surface mapping to address these shortcomings. WHU-Road3D provides approximately 100 km of diverse Chinese highways and urban streets. It features a rich combination of MLS point clouds, associated panoramic images, vehicle trajectories, detailed vectorized annotations, and pointwise instance annotations for road surface elements. The dataset carefully annotates 15 distinct categories of road elements, including common markings like road boundaries, zebra crossings, lane dividers, stop lines, as well as 10 types of direction symbols. To our knowledge, WHU-Road3D stands as the first multimodal dataset for mapping such a comprehensive range of road surface elements, designed to facilitate cutting-edge research in instance segmentation, full-element extraction and mapping on road surfaces. Beyond these immediate applications, WHU-Road3D also provides a valuable source for hierarchical road topology reasoning, map updating, and street scene generation. The dataset, along with the code for our benchmark methods, will be made publicly available at \href{https://github.com/WHU-USI3DV/WHU-Road3D.git}{https://github.com/WHU-USI3DV/WHU-Road3D.git}.
